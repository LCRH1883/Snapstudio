Clean up your Android gallery with Snap Swipe, a free photo cleaner that lets you review your photos one at a time. Snap Swipe is completely free, with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Swipe to keep or delete
In Swipe to choose mode, swipe right to keep a photo, left to delete it, or up to share it. You can also use the on-screen controls to keep or delete photos.

Clear out photo clutter
Sort through blurry shots, old screenshots, and extra copies. Delete photos as you go or queue them for batch deletion. You decide what stays in your gallery.

Review at your pace
Start with your newest photos or work from oldest to newest. Choose between swipe review and scroll mode, and use light or dark appearance.

Jump to a date or resume later
Open the photo timeline to browse from oldest to newest. Use the calendar to jump to a year, month, or day with photos. If you leave partway through a cleanup, resume your saved review when you return.

Photos stay on your device
Snap Swipe reviews photos locally and does not upload them. When you share a photo, you choose where to send it through Android’s share menu.
