## Asset sources

The screenshots were captured from Android emulator builds at commits `f880fac` (1.3.0) and `d475330` (1.4.0). The 1080 × 1920 JPEG exports use actual app content. The graphics crop the Android status strip and add a headline and background outside the app screen.

The emulator used sample landscape photos under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Source photos include [Yosemite meadow](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1472396961693-142e6e269027), [alpine lake](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1501785888041-af3ef285b470), [lakeside lodge](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1470770841072-f978cf4d019e), [forest river](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1473448912268-2022ce9509d8), [coastal sunset](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1473116763249-2faaef81ccda), and [mountain pass](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1464822759023-fed622ff2c3b). The sample dates were assigned in the emulator library to demonstrate the timeline.
